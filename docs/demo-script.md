# TrailLens — Demo Video Script (60–120 Seconds)

> **Theme:** Touch Grass — AI that helps you look away from the screen.  
> **Target Duration:** 90–110 seconds.  

---

## Shot List & Sequence

| Timestamp | Visual | Narration / Audio | On-Screen Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:10** | Person walking in a sunny park with their head bowed down, scrolling an endless social feed. | *"Every app on your phone wants one thing: your permanent visual attention. Even AI apps keep you trapped in endless chats."* | Camera zooms out to show vibrant trees and flowers being completely ignored by the user. |
| **0:10 – 0:25** | Explorer stops near a blooming wild flower, opens **TrailLens** on mobile browser. | *"Meet TrailLens. An outdoor AI companion built to get you off the screen and into the world."* | Explorer opens TrailLens, taps **[ Choose or Take Photo ]**, and snaps a photo of a dandelion. |
| **0:25 – 0:45** | Screen shows TrailLens analyzing via open-weight AI. Results card appears. | *"Powered by Google Gemma 2 open weights, TrailLens doesn't chat. It gives you a concise identification, an honest confidence score, and three physical details to look for with your own eyes."* | UI reveals: *"Common Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale)"*, confidence badge (88%), and checklist: leaf lobes, stem latex, pollinator presence. |
| **0:45 – 0:60** | User taps **"Listen"**; calming audio plays. Then taps **"Step Away / Put Phone Away"**. | *ElevenLabs voice cue plays:* *"You may be looking at a Common Dandelion. Notice the jagged leaf lobes. Now put your phone away."* | Fullscreen **Step Away Mode** opens. Circular timer starts at 3:00. Subtle 432Hz ambient chime sounds. |
| **0:60 – 0:80** | Explorer slides phone deep into their pocket. Kneels down to examine the real flower. | *Ambient outdoor sounds (wind rustling, birds chirping).* | The explorer kneels in the grass, gently inspects the stem and petals, and smiles up at the sky. Zero screens visible. |
| **0:80 – 1:00** | 3 minutes later, explorer takes phone out, taps **"I Observed This"** and records a note. | *"Once you've experienced nature directly, you return to save your finding to your offline field journal, synced to MongoDB Atlas."* | Explorer taps **"Save Observation"**; note appears in Field Journal with timestamp and sync badge. |
| **1:00 – 1:15** | Quick cut to desktop screen showing TabPFN activity predictor and Render dashboard. | *"Under the hood: open-weight Gemma architecture, TabPFN tabular prediction for optimal trail activities, and a production FastAPI backend on Render with zero secret leakage."* | Brief montage of TabPFN weather sliders, MongoDB Atlas sync indicator, and passing GitHub CI tests. |
| **1:15 – 1:20** | Hero shot of sunlight filtering through green tree leaves. Tagline text overlay. | *"TrailLens: AI that helps you look away from the screen."* | **TRAILLENS**<br/>*See something. Understand it. Step away.* |

---

## Production Tips for Video Recording
- **Filming Location:** A quiet community park, nature reserve, or sunlit backyard with real plants.
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight with high contrast to showcase the outdoor readability of the TrailLens UI.
- **Microphone:** Lapel mic to capture natural outdoor audio alongside the narrator's voice.
